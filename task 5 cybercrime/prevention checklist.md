Stay Safe Online: A Checklist for Indian College Students
College life in India involves heavy use of digital tools, from attending online classes to using UPI for canteen payments. Staying secure requires both awareness and action.

UPI & Financial Safety (Mandatory)
Pin Protection Knowledge: Always remember that you NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. If someone asks you to enter your PIN to "accept a refund" or "collect a prize," it is a scam.

Verify Merchant Names: Before hitting 'Pay' on apps like GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm, double-check the actual name registered to the QR code. Scammers often paste fake QR codes over legitimate ones in busy market areas.

Password & Account Security
Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Activate 2FA on your university email, WhatsApp, and Instagram. Even if someone steals your password, they won't be able to log in without the code sent to your phone.

Avoid Campus Wi-Fi for Banking: Public or hostel Wi-Fi networks can be intercepted. Never log into your bank account or enter credit card details while connected to open campus Wi-Fi; use your mobile data instead.

 
Beware of 'Urgent' WhatsApp Requests: If a friend or family member messages you asking for an urgent UPI transfer because of an "emergency," call them directly first. Their account might have been hacked.

Check Privacy Settings: Set your social media profiles (especially Instagram and Facebook) to 'Private'. This prevents identity thieves from using your photos to create deepfakes or fake profiles in your name.


Avoid Pirated Study Materials: Do not download "cracked" versions of expensive software or textbooks from unverified Telegram channels. These files often contain Malware or Ransomware that can lock your laptop.

Update Your OS Immediately: Whenever your phone or laptop suggests a system update, install it. These updates often contain "security patches" that fix vulnerabilities hackers use to get into your device.
